# Complete the Adventure

Your goal is to demonstrate that the finished player can complete {{ game_name }} and leave behind evidence that the solution is reliable. This final outcome proves that the individual MCP, A2A, retrieval, memory, and observability capabilities work together.

## Challenge

Run the complete player through the published quest, investigate any failures using the session log, and refine the agent until it reaches the end without bypassing specialist or game-server responsibilities.

## Expected outcome

- The agent starts or resumes the correct player.
- Travel and question missions use the appropriate specialists.
- The quest reaches its completed state.
- The player appears on the leaderboard.
- A session log captures the successful run.
- The implementation contains no embedded credentials or game answers.

## Completion check

Show the completed game state, leaderboard entry, persisted player memory, and session log. Be ready to explain how MCP, A2A, retrieval, memory, and logging each contributed to the result.
