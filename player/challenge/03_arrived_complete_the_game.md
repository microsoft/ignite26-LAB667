# Arrived: Complete the Game

One final journey remains. Ask Agent42 for help again, submit the recommended
route to the game, and arrive at the Open Hack location.

## Objectives

1. Resume the authenticated player and inspect the final movement mission.
2. Send the complete final origin and destination to Agent42.
3. Review the recommendation and submit its supported transport answer and
   route reference to MCP.
4. Continue until MCP reports that the quest is complete.
5. Confirm the completed state on the Player timeline and leaderboard.

## Rules

- Reuse the existing authenticated MCP and Agent42 clients.
- Let MCP decide whether the route answer completes the game.
- Do not bypass the final movement mission or manually inject progress.
- Investigate failures without exposing credentials.

## Completion check

The Player timeline shows the final Agent42 advice and arrival, MCP reports a
completed quest, and the nickname appears on the {{ game_name }} leaderboard.
